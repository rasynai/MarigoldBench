# Bioanalytical method note (extract)

Calibration range 0.5-50 nM, seven levels, plasma matrix.
Regression: when the calibrated range spans two or more orders of
magnitude and the residual scatter is proportional to concentration,
the curve must be fitted with 1/x^2 weighting. Unweighted fitting is
acceptable only across a single decade.

Standards prepared from a stock other than the qualified stock lot
for the batch are not eligible for inclusion in the regression.

Lower limit of quantitation (LLOQ): 1.0 nM. Concentrations below the
LLOQ are not quantifiable and must not be released as numeric
results; they are reported qualitatively.

Matrix: plasma. This method has been validated in plasma only.
