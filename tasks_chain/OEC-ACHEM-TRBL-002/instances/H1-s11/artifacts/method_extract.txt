QC-LAB-214, rev. 6 — specified impurity assay (extract)

Range: 0.020 to 10.0 percent w/w as Impurity A.
Reporting threshold: 0.020 percent w/w.
Values below the reporting threshold are issued as <0.020 percent;
a numerical value below that threshold is not placed on the CoA.

Calibration has approximately constant percent RSD across the range.
Use linear regression with fitted intercept and weights 1/x^2.
Unweighted regression may be used for diagnostics but not release.

Potential single-point anomalies are evaluated within a replicate set
using the two-sided Grubbs statistic. At n=5 and alpha=0.05, Gcrit=1.715.
Do not remove a point unless the statistic exceeds the critical value
and an assignable laboratory cause is documented.

Assess lack of fit on the required weighted regression by separating
weighted pure error from weighted regression residual error.
For this design, df_LOF=7 and df_PE=20; Fcrit at alpha=0.05 is 2.51.

Global diagnostic checks: r-squared at least 0.995 and recovery at
the 0.500 percent level from 90.0 to 110.0 percent. These checks do
not replace the prescribed weighting, anomaly test, or lack-of-fit test.

The calibration is specific to Impurity A. Other impurity peaks require
individually established relative response factors before summation.
