# Bioanalytical method note (extract)

Calibration range 0.2-50 nM, seven levels, plasma matrix. The two
lowest levels anchor the curve below the reporting limit.
Calibration standards must be prepared from a stock lot qualified
for the study on the certificate of the batch record.

The validated calibration treatments for this method are unweighted
linear regression and 1/x^2-weighted linear regression, considered
in that order. A treatment is acceptable only if every
back-calculated standard in the fit falls within 15% of its nominal
concentration (20% at the lowest fitted level); the first treatment
in the validated order that meets the limits is used. Treatments
outside the validated pair are not permitted for release.

Lower limit of quantitation (LLOQ): 1.0 nM. Concentrations below the
LLOQ are not quantifiable and must not be released as numeric
results; they are reported qualitatively.

Matrix: plasma. This method has been validated in plasma only.
