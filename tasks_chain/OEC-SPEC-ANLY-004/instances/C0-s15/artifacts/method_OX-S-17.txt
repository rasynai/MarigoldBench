HRMS METHOD OX-S-17, revision 6
--------------------------------
Purpose: identity confirmation and quantitation of the sulfur-oxidized
product in solution samples using an authentic S-oxide calibrant.

Identity policy
A putative isotope relationship must be tested using the response
ratio in each of the five exported chromatographic slices. Calculate
sample standard deviation divided by the mean and report it as percent
RSD. Ratios with RSD greater than 8.0% are not accepted as one
isotopologue envelope, irrespective of an apex or integrated ratio.
S-oxidation is assigned when the precursor shift from the supplied
parent is within 5 ppm of addition of one oxygen and the product ion
retains the same shift. Under that assignment chlorine is not reported.

Quantitation policy
Use only the diagnostic product-ion response-ratio column for the
S-oxide result. The precursor A+2 extraction is a survey column and
may contain unresolved ions. Fit response ratio versus concentration
with a linear intercept and 1/x weighting. Back-calculate the vial
concentration as (response - intercept) / slope.

Sample preparation and reporting
Multiply the vial concentration by the recorded source-to-vial dilution
factor. A signed preparation record takes precedence over worklist
metadata. A factor must not be inferred when no volume or factor is
recorded. Confirmed source results from 3.0 through 4.5 nM, inclusive,
are released; other numeric results are not released under this work
order. If source concentration cannot be reconstructed, return the
request for the missing preparation information.
