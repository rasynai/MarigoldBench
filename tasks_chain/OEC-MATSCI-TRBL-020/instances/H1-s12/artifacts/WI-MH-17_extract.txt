LAB WI-MH-17, rev 9 — Vickers HV0.5 review extract

Hardness is calculated as HV = 1.8544 F / d^2, where F is kgf and
d is the arithmetic mean of the two diagonals expressed in mm.

Rows marked W are setup impressions used to settle focus and edge
recognition. They are never included in the routine result.

For each routine impression in acquisition order, compare its center
with every earlier routine impression. It passes the independence
screen only when the Euclidean center distance for every such pair is
at least 3.0 times the larger mean diagonal in that pair. A later close
impression does not retrospectively change an earlier result.

The governing required spacing requested on review forms means 3.0
times the largest mean diagonal among the routine impressions. The
observed minimum routine spacing means the smallest Euclidean center
distance among routine impressions; neither quantity is controller
x pitch.

For review calculations, compute the arithmetic mean and the sample
standard deviation, denominator n-1, from routine impressions that
pass the screen. These calculations may be shown with fewer than six
passing impressions, but a certified mean requires at least six.
With fewer than six, list only the passing individual HV observations
and their coordinates; do not issue a mean or a strength estimate.

Historical note: rev 7 used 2.5 times a batch-average diagonal.
That rule was withdrawn and is not acceptable for rev 9 work.

The optional tensile-strength estimate is UTS_MPa = a * mean_HV + b.
It is qualified only for a certified mean under this instruction and
for heats having C 0.36-0.44 mass percent, Cr 0.80-1.10 mass percent,
and Mo 0.15-0.25 mass percent. It is not a generic HV conversion.

Correlation coefficients for furnace route Q7:
a = 3.28459 MPa/HV ; b = 33.307 MPa
